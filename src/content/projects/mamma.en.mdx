## Care records as conversation context

Mamma reduces the need to repeatedly explain a child's situation. Child-specific feeding, sleep, diaper, and growth records provide context for MammaTalk alongside the child's age.

## Current features

- **Quick logs:** More than 12 care categories, including temperature and medication
- **Timers:** Track ongoing feeding and sleep sessions
- **Reports:** Height, weight, and care statistics over time
- **Development:** Age-based checklists
- **Multiple children:** Separate profiles and records
- **MammaTalk:** AI conversations grounded in saved records

## Web and mobile architecture

The product uses a Next.js, React, and TypeScript web app with an AWS Amplify Gen2 backend. TanStack Query manages server state. An Expo native shell connects the web product to the iOS and Android experience.
