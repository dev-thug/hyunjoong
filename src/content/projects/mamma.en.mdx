# Mamma - Data-Driven Parenting Agent

**A data-driven parenting app from daily logging to AI coaching**

Mamma helps parents capture repetitive childcare events with minimal effort, then turns those records into practical guidance through AI analysis. Instead of being just a tracking app, it acts more like an operational hub for daily parenting decisions.

## 💡 Key Outcomes

- **Build-to-launch speed:** A 2-person team shipped the MVP in 3 days with vibe coding and launched on the App Store in 7 days
- **Low-friction daily logging:** Quickly log feeding, sleep, diaper, medication, temperature, and activities with a daily summary dashboard
- **AI-powered personalized insights:** Analyze feeding/sleep/development patterns to provide context-aware guidance
- **Structured growth management:** Track milestones with an AI composite score and age-specific checklists
- **Local activity discovery:** Find nearby kids cafes, playgrounds, museums, and culture centers with map-based search
- **Warm community interactions:** Exchange practical tips and emotional support with other caregivers
- **24/7 AI coach:** Use MammaTalk for real-time Q&A, photo-based questions, and tailored recommendations

## 🏗 Product Structure

**1. Logging & Summary Layer**

- Capture core parenting events in a structured way and review them as a daily snapshot
- Reduce missed records by minimizing repeated input steps

**2. Analysis & Decision Layer**

- Transform accumulated data into personalized insights for sleep, feeding, and development
- Focus on actionable next steps, not just static charts

**3. Local + Social Layer**

- Use map-based discovery to plan outings faster
- Combine peer community support with AI coaching for both practical and emotional needs

## 🛠 Key Features

Quick logging, AI smart analysis, AI growth management, local play map, parenting community, MammaTalk AI coach
