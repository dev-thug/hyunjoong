**A mobile product that connects repetitive childcare logs to useful next steps**

Mamma is designed to help parents record daily childcare events and review accumulated context before deciding what to do next. It connects logging, AI-assisted summaries, growth checklists, local-place discovery, and community interaction in one workflow.

## Core Capabilities

- **Low-friction logging:** Structure feeding, sleep, diaper, medication, temperature, and activity records
- **Summaries and patterns:** Review daily snapshots and recurring patterns across accumulated logs
- **Growth checklists:** Keep age-specific checkpoints alongside daily records
- **Local discovery:** Find nearby kids cafes, playgrounds, museums, and culture centers
- **Community:** Exchange practical experience and information through feeds and comments
- **AI assistance:** Organize questions and suggested follow-up checks from user-provided context

## Product Structure

**1. Logging & Summary Layer**

- Store core childcare events in a consistent structure
- Present daily snapshots designed to reduce the effort of reviewing raw logs

**2. Analysis & Decision Layer**

- Summarize sleep, feeding, and activity flows
- Focus on useful follow-up checks instead of static charts alone

**3. Local & Community Layer**

- Organize outing candidates through map-based place discovery
- Provide a space for parents in similar situations to exchange experience and information

## Key Features

Quick logging, AI-assisted summaries, growth checklists, local play map, parenting community, and contextual question support
