## A conversation with a next scene

Petty is less about asking a question and receiving an answer than continuing a story with a character. A consistent voice, a recognizable setting, and a connection to earlier conversations help make the next scene worth discovering.

I am building the journey from finding a character to chatting and returning to saved history. Planning, design, development, and operations are handled independently.

## Starting with a character

Users choose a character and story, then begin a conversation within that setting. They can return to previous conversations or regenerate a response to explore another direction.

Reply suggestions and choices provide a way forward when it is difficult to decide what to say. They use recent conversation context and accumulated memory rather than unrelated prompts.

## Fast delivery, comfortable reading

Conversation generation uses an OpenRouter-backed model. Lambda verifies authentication and conversation ownership before sending content over SSE, then persists the completed message and state.

Model output and reading speed are different concerns. The UI has a separate display queue: the beginning appears immediately, and the rest continues at a readable pace. Server persistence and completion do not wait for that animation.

This lets me address time to first text separately from the problem of a large block arriving all at once.

## Keeping longer conversations coherent

A long history cannot all be sent to the model unchanged. Recent messages are combined with accumulated memory, and regeneration preserves previous response versions.

Output language and speaker markers are checked separately. These checks help reduce unexpected language changes and keep displayed text aligned with what is saved.

Failed or interrupted turns remain distinct from completed conversations, and usage handling follows those states. The experience depends on reliable history and state as well as generated prose.

## Shared foundations for web and mobile

The app uses a Next.js static export and Capacitor, connected to an AWS serverless data and authentication backend. TanStack Query and Zustand separate server data from screen state.

Provider calls and transport sit behind a conversation contract. Screens work with messages and stream events, while adapters connect models and persistence. Changing a model should not require rewriting the conversation UI.
